🔒 Highest Privacy for Renters & Owners

Your Mobile Number & Address Are Never Public

We know the biggest hesitation in lending or renting from neighbours is privacy. On Rent Anything, both the renter’s and the owner’s personal details are strictly protected at every step.

📵 Zero Spam Calls

Mobile Number Hidden Until You Approve

Strangers browsing the map can never see your phone number or WhatsApp. Your number is shared only with the specific person whose rental request you explicitly approve.

🏠 No Doorstep Strangers

Exact Home Address Is Never Shown

We never display your flat number, building name, or exact GPS pin. The map only shows a rough 150-metre neighbourhood zone so nobody knows which house is yours.

✅ 100% In Your Control

Review Profile Before Approving

When someone requests your item (or responds to your Wanted Pin), you see their verified name, community rating, and dates first. If you decline, they never see your contact info.

☕ Safe Handover Spots

Meet at a Metro Station or Café

Even after you approve a request, you never have to share your home address. Hand over the item at a nearby Metro Station, Third Wave Coffee, or your society gate.
