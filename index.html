🧭 Product Philosophy

8 Non-Negotiable Design Principles

Engineered from the ground up for hyperlocal, short-term peer-to-peer consumer item rentals with zero friction.

Principle #01

Map IS the Product

No bloated landing page, no onboarding carousel. The interactive map full of price pins IS the very first screen.

Principle #02

Zero Friction to Browse

No login, no app install, no invasive permissions. Tap a WhatsApp link → browse the live map in under 3 seconds.

Principle #03

List in Under 2 Minutes

Minimal required fields, smart defaults, client-side browser photo compression, and 1-tap GPS pre-fill.

Principle #04

One Neighbourhood at a Time

Never launch city-wide with sparse pins. High pin density in HSR Layout or Indiranagar beats thin coverage across Bengaluru.

Principle #05

Community First, Business Later

No platform fees, no ads, and no premium tiers for at least 9 months until organic neighbourhood habit is proven.

Principle #06

Privacy by Default (DPDP)

Exact coordinates rounded to ±150m before storage. Contact details revealed only after mutual request acceptance.

Principle #07

Cost Near Zero (<₹3,000/mo)

Every architectural choice optimises for free tiers (Supabase, MapLibre, Vercel, Resend) to survive without premature commissions.

Principle #08

Mobile-First, Not Mobile-Only

Engineered for a ₹12,000 Android phone on 4G (360px viewport, ≥44px touch targets, <1.5s First Contentful Paint).

Why Past P2P Rental Startups Failed (Rentomo, RentSher, Fat Llama) Our Rent-Anything Counter-Strategy Key Platform Advantages
1. Cold-Start / Empty Map: Launched city-wide; users saw zero items near them and bounced. Micro-Neighbourhood Density: Seed 100–150 items by hand in ONE area before expanding. Flexible Pricing: Short-term daily & weekly rates + refundable security deposit clarity.
2. Trust Gap: Handing a ₹50k camera to a stranger without protection felt risky. Start with Low/Mid-Value Gear: Drills, tents, projectors + condition photos, ratings & UPI deposits. Live Availability: Interactive 30-day booking calendar on every item card.
3. Logistics Friction: High coordination effort for a ₹300/day item. Hyperlocal (<1.5 km) + Safe Spots: Meet at nearby Metro stations or cafés; WhatsApp 1-click connect. Demand Signal: Orange Wanted Pins ("Need projector Oct 20–22") with 5km daily owner email digest.
4. Premature Monetisation & High Fees: Charging 20–25% commission killed liquidity. Near-Zero Cost (<₹3k/mo): 100% free for 9+ months. No employees, no inventory, community-driven. Privacy-First Reveal: Contact info is revealed only after the owner accepts a structured date request.
⚙️ Built With Modern Zero-Cost Tools

System Architecture (<₹3,000/Month at Scale)

Every tool mentioned in our specification is chosen to deliver sub-3-second mobile performance on 4G while keeping infrastructure costs near zero.

Next.js (App Router) + React Vercel Free Tier

SSR for SEO browse pages (/rent/cameras/indiranagar) and fast CSR for the interactive map & PWA offline service worker shell.

MapLibre GL JS + Vector Tiles Open Source

Drop-in open-source WebGL map rendering, dynamic price pins, zoom-level clustering (≤15 zoom), and smooth pan/zoom on budget Android phones.

Supabase + PostgreSQL + PostGIS Free Tier (500MB)

Powers geospatial queries (ST_DWithin 5km radius alerts, ST_MakeEnvelope viewport queries), Row-Level Security (RLS), and Realtime badges.

Supabase Auth & Storage 50k MAU Free

Google OAuth 1-tap + Magic Link verification. Client-side browser image compression uploads directly to S3-compatible Supabase Storage CDN.

Supabase Edge Functions + Resend 3,000 Emails/mo Free

Deno edge functions + pg_cron trigger instant request emails, post-rental return reminders, and daily 5km Wanted Pin digests via Resend.

Nominatim / Photon + Plausible Cookie-Free DPDP

Free open-source geocoding for Bengaluru neighbourhood search (<500ms) paired with privacy-first, cookie-less Plausible/Umami analytics.
