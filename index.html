How Rent Anything Works in 3 Simple Steps

Here is what the upcoming Rent Anything app looks like — built so you can find what you need near you, book it in seconds, and pick it up safely in your neighbourhood.

Step 01 · Explore Nearby

1. Open the Map & Spot Items Near You

Instantly see what neighbours around you are lending (📷 ₹600/day, ⛺ ₹350/day, 🔧 ₹200/day) or post an orange Wanted Pin (🔍 Need Projector) if you are looking for something specific.

No Login to Browse Live Price Pins Post Wanted Pins
Step 02 · Transparent Details

2. Check Availability & Request in One Tap

Tap any pin to view real condition photos, daily or weekly rates, refundable deposit, distance from you (e.g., HSR Layout · 1.2 km away), neighbour ratings (4.8★), and available dates.

30-Day Calendar Verified Ratings 1-Tap Request
Step 03 · Privacy & Pickup

3. Safe Privacy Circle & Easy Handover

Your exact flat or house address is never shown on the map. Once the owner accepts your request, you both connect on WhatsApp and meet at a convenient nearby spot like a Metro Station or café.
